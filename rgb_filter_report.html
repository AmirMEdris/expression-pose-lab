Watch this filter Dream

Live Dream maximizes the selected channel’s mean across spatial positions. The examples above rank its strongest spatial unit. These are related but different objectives. A larger response does not establish that a generated pattern is an eye or mouth.

Dataset and evidence limits

Source images and detected duplicate groups were kept within one data split. Identities may overlap, near-duplicates can be missed, and labels may be noisy. Validation is used for inspection; it is not a fresh generalization test. No camera images are included.

This report contains natural examples for every filter. It does not include the three-seed optimization tests from the older grayscale explorer. The separate Dream-progress report compares fixed validation inputs across selected training checkpoints.
